Turning randomness into function

A nanophotonics researcher specializing in photonic security, disordered photonics, metasurfaces, semiconductor nanostructures, and integrated photonic devices. Work translates concepts into experimentally demonstrated technologies through advanced nanofabrication and optical characterization, built across research groups in India and Europe and supported by competitive fellowships including an MSCA postdoctoral award.

2024 — present

Postdoctoral Researcher

Istituto Italiano di Tecnologia (IIT), Genova · PI: Prof. Roman Krahne, Optoelectronics

Leads two projects: biodegradable physically unclonable functions (PUFs) for optical security, and hybrid integration of layered perovskites with Si₃N₄ photonic structures for controlling light–matter interactions — spanning device design, nanofabrication, optical characterization, and photonic integration.

2021 — 2023

Postdoctoral Researcher

University of Twente, The Netherlands · PI: Prof. Pepijn Pinkse, Adaptive Quantum Optics

Pioneered PUF keys on a photonic-chip platform, designing mirror-symmetric photonic media via FDTD modeling and fabricating them with direct laser writing — producing unique, near-impossible-to-replicate identifiers verified through rigorous optical characterization.

2013 — 2015

Junior Project Fellow

CSIR-CEERI, Pilani, India · PI: Dr. Ajay Agarwal (MEMS), Dr. Pankaj B. Agarwal (SNTG)

Developed advanced security features and substrates for Indian currency notes, fabricating metasurfaces via Dip Pen Nanolithography and characterizing their optical performance for anti-counterfeiting.
