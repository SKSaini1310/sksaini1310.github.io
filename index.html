Profile

Turning randomness into function

I am a nanophotonics researcher specializing in photonic security, disordered photonics, metasurfaces, semiconductor nanostructures, and integrated photonic devices. My work translates concepts into experimentally demonstrated technologies through advanced nanofabrication and optical characterization, I have developed this expertise across research groups in India and Europe and supported by competitive fellowships including a Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions Postdoctoral Fellowship.

Postdoctoral Researcher

Leads two projects: biodegradable physically unclonable functions (PUFs) for optical security, and hybrid integration of layered perovskites with Si₃N₄ photonic structures for controlling light–matter interactions — spanning device design, nanofabrication, optical characterization, and photonic integration.

Postdoctoral Researcher

University of Twente, The Netherlands · PI: Prof. Pepijn Pinkse, Adaptive Quantum Optics

Pioneered PUF keys on a photonic-chip platform, designing mirror-symmetric photonic media via FDTD modeling and fabricating them with direct laser writing — producing unique, near-impossible-to-replicate identifiers verified through rigorous optical characterization.

Developed advanced security features and substrates for Indian currency notes, fabricating metasurfaces via Dip Pen Nanolithography and characterizing their optical performance for anti-counterfeiting.

Research

Projects

01

Biodegradable physically unclonable functions

Prof. Roman Krahne, Istituto Italiano di Tecnologia

Designing biodegradable PUFs for optical security: unique, hard-to-replicate optical identifiers that leave no lasting waste.

02

Layered perovskites on Si₃N₄ photonic structures

Prof. Roman Krahne, Istituto Italiano di Tecnologia

Hybrid integration of 2D layered perovskites with silicon-nitride photonic crystals to control light–matter coupling on chip.

03

Mirror-symmetric multiple-scattering media

Prof. Pepijn Pinkse, University of Twente

FDTD-designed, direct-laser-written photonic media with engineered symmetry, giving PUF keys on a photonic chip platform. Published as a Physical Review Letters cover article.

04

Bio-inspired disordered photonic structures

Dr. Rajesh V. Nair, IIT Ropar (PhD)

Light transport and emission in disordered silicon nanowires, nanopyramids and photonic structures with short-range order, inspired by butterfly wing scales.

05

Metasurface security features for banknotes

Dr. Ajay Agarwal and Dr. Pankaj B. Agarwal, CSIR-CEERI

Dip Pen Nanolithography metasurfaces on varied substrates for anti-counterfeiting patterns on Indian currency notes.
